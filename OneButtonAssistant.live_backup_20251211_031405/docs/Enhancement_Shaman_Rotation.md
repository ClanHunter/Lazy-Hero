# Enhancement Shaman Rotation in The War Within

Enhancement Shaman can be overwhelming at first glance, due to the amount of buttons and effects it has pulling you in different directions. This means you need to carefully navigate the talent tree to build a suitable rotation. Below are 3 key rules for success when playing:

## How to Play Enhancement Shaman

Enhancement Shaman operates on a strict priority list, rather than with a traditional resource system. That means all of your actions will be used in an ordered list of highest value from top to bottom, judged each GCD. This means you need to be aware of your current state throughout an encounter. It tends to follow these 3 key rules:

1. Avoid Maelstrom Weapon waste - this serves as our pseudo-resource, and caps at 10 when using Raging Maelstrom. This powers our strongest "spender" abilities such as `spell:403` (Lightning Bolt) and `spell:421` (Chain Lightning), so waste as little as possible.
2. Keep Abilities on cooldown - as we have no main resource, abilities are instead limited by cooldowns. That means main strikes like `spell:17364` (Stormstrike) and `spell:60103` (Lava Lash) need to be cycled as often as possible, all while trying to avoid clashes.
3. React to procs - both hero talents have key procs to react to whenever they occur. Totemic has Hot Hand and Molten Thunder, while Stormbringer has Supercharge and Static Accumulation, each mixing up resource state.

## Hero Talent Choices

Due to Enhancement having two extremely rotationally impactful Hero Talents in Stormbringer and Totemic, which you pick dictates many of your other build choices, alongside your playstyle. Both Hero Trees are supported on this page in their respective tabs in the Opener, Single Target and AoE sections.

The current recommendations in Season 3 are:

- Single Target: Stormbringer
- Raid Cleave: Stormbringer
- Mythic+: Stormbringer

After gaining access to the Season 3 Tier Set, Stormbringer catapults ahead of Totemic in all situations. From a performance perspective, once you have it there's no reason to ever switch Hero Trees once you have it. This extends to both Raid and Mythic+ situations.

## Best Enhancement Shaman Opener

The Enhancement Shaman opener is heavily dictated by your chosen hero talent. They each have sequenced setups to get things started, but share a similar goal - get maintenance effects rolling so you can have as little friction as possible during cooldowns. With Stormbringer selected, opener details are as follows:

The Stormbringer opener aims to get into `spell:114051` (Ascendance) as quickly as possible so it can begin cycling Maelstrom Weapon to build momentum for Tempest procs. Depending on other picks, all setup will be done before this so you can focus entirely on Windstrike and efficiently managing resources.

- Pre-cast `spell:117014` (Elemental Blast) 2 seconds before the pull
- Cast `spell:8050` (Flame Shock) on the way into melee
- Cast `spell:17364` (Stormstrike)
- Cast `spell:342240` (Ice Strike)
- Cast `spell:51533` (Feral Spirit)
- Cast `spell:384352` (Doom Winds)
- Cast `spell:114051` (Ascendance)
- Cast Windstrike on cooldown — continue with normal priority

## Best Enhancement Shaman Single Target Rotation

Enhancement in Single Target plays around a priority list, juggling both ability cooldowns and resources based on an order of power. This means with each GCD we go top to bottom on a list, and press the button with the current highest value. With Stormbringer selected, the priority is as follows:

- Cast `spell:51533` (Feral Spirit)
- Use Windstrike on cooldown during `spell:114051` (Ascendance)
- Cast `spell:384352` (Doom Winds)
- Cast `spell:114051` (Ascendance)
- Cast Maelstrom Weapon spenders at 8+ stacks — Priority: `spell:117014` (Elemental Blast) > `spell:403` (Lightning Bolt) > `spell:421` (Chain Lightning)
- Cast `spell:17364` (Stormstrike) if at 2 charges
- Cast Voltaic Blaze (proc-driven filler)
- Cast `spell:342240` (Ice Strike)
- Cast `spell:60103` (Lava Lash)
- Cast `spell:197214` (Sundering) when appropriate
- Use `spell:403` (Lightning Bolt) when you have sufficient Maelstrom Weapon stacks
- Use `spell:8056` (Frost Shock) or `spell:8050` (Flame Shock) to fill

Notes: Stormbringer focuses on Maelstrom Weapon cycling to reach Tempest procs. Spend at 8+ stacks when possible; during `spell:114051` every GCD should be spent on Windstrike.

## Best Enhancement Shaman AoE Rotation

In AoE, Enhancement gains a lot of momentum due to the increased Maelstrom Weapon generation provided by `spell:187874` (Crash Lightning). We shift to a multi-target priority at 2 targets, and both Hero Trees differ significantly on where damage comes from.

- Cast `spell:8050` (Flame Shock) if it isn't active with `spell:326059` (Primordial Wave) available
- Cast `spell:60103` (Lava Lash) to spread Flame Shock
- Cast `spell:326059` (Primordial Wave) with Flame Shock spread
- Use Windstrike on cooldown during `spell:114051` (Ascendance)
- Cast `spell:384352` (Doom Winds)
- Cast `spell:326059` (Primordial Wave) at 10 Maelstrom Weapon stacks
- Cast Maelstrom Weapon spenders at 7+ stacks — Priority: `spell:421` (Chain Lightning) > `spell:403` (Lightning Bolt)
- Cast `spell:187874` (Crash Lightning)
- Cast Stormstrike / `spell:17364` and generators: `spell:342240` (Ice Strike) / `spell:60103` (Lava Lash)
- Cast `spell:8050` (Flame Shock) to refresh or `spell:8056` (Frost Shock) to fill

Notes: With Stormbringer, cycle Maelstrom Weapon aggressively; `spell:326059` (Primordial Wave) needs Flame Shock setup every 30s for Conductive Energy.

## Is The Single Button Rotation Assistant Good for Enhancement Shaman?

The assist tool helps with learning; Totemic tends to be executed better by strict priority assistants, while Stormbringer needs more nuanced Maelstrom Weapon management. If using the tool, Totemic is generally recommended for learning.
